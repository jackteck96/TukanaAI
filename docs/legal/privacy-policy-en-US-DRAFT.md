- Providing document management services

**Compliance with a legal obligation (LGPD Art. 7, II)**
- Meeting legal and regulatory requirements
- Responding to court orders

**Consent (LGPD Art. 7, I)**
- Sending marketing communications
- Sharing with partners (when applicable)
- Data analysis for improvements (optional)

**Legitimate interest (LGPD Art. 7, IX)**
- Platform security and fraud prevention
- Improving the services we offer
- Credit protection

### 4. Data Sharing

Your personal data may be shared with:

**Service providers:**
- Supabase (database infrastructure)
- Resend (email delivery)
- Google (authentication and calendar)

All vendors are carefully vetted and are contractually bound to protect
your data.

**Competent authorities:** when required by law or court order.

**Important:** We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with
third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

### 5. Security Measures

We implement robust technical and organizational measures:

- Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Row-Level Security (RLS) on all tables
- Audit logs for all access to personal data
- Regular backups and a disaster-recovery plan
- Continuous security monitoring
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) available

### 6. Data Retention

**Registration and contract data:** Kept for the duration of the
contract and for 5 years afterward, as required by Brazilian accounting
and tax law.

**Uploaded documents:** Kept as agreed contractually or as required by
applicable law.

**Access logs:** Kept for 6 months for security and audit purposes,
unless a longer legal retention period applies.

**Marketing data:** Kept until consent is withdrawn or for 2 years
without interaction.

### 7. Your Rights (LGPD Art. 18)

As a data subject, you have the following rights:

- **Confirmation and access:** confirm whether we process your data and
  access it
- **Correction:** correct incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data
- **Anonymization, blocking, or deletion:** request anonymization or
  deletion of unnecessary or excessive data
- **Portability:** receive your data in a structured, interoperable
  format
- **Deletion:** request deletion of data processed on the basis of
  consent
- **Information about sharing:** know who we share your data with
- **Information about the right to refuse consent** and its
  consequences
- **Withdrawal of consent:** withdraw your consent at any time

**To exercise your rights:**
1. Go to your profile and open "My Personal Data"
2. Or contact our DPO: dpo@fuzen.app

We will respond to your request within 15 days, per LGPD Art. 18 §3.

### 9. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (such as Supabase) may store data on

- The level of protection is equivalent to what LGPD requires

### 10. Security Incident Notification

In the event of a security incident that could pose a relevant risk or
harm to data subjects, per LGPD Art. 48:

- We will notify the ANPD (Brazil's data protection authority) within a
  reasonable time
- We will notify you by email or via the platform
- We will explain the nature of the incident and the technical measures
  taken
- We will provide guidance on protecting your data
